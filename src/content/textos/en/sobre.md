titulo: About FDM
---

The Laboratory of Philosophy of Law and Justice in Motion (FDM) is a research group of the Department of Procedural Law and Propaedeutics (DEPRO), within the Centre for Applied Social Sciences (CCSA) of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte. The group starts from the idea that the philosophy of law has a practical dimension and invites a reassessment of law through reflection on justice.

Research is organised in three lines and can proceed in three ways: the history of philosophy, the study of a particular philosopher or author, and philosophising itself. The theme of the first year of activities is Liberty, Equality and Inclusion, which the coordinator relates to Kant's thought, to German idealism and to the philosophical tradition that projects human rights into the present.
